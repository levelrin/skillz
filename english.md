## Conclusion First

Write information deductively. In other words, write your conclusion first. After that, add details to support your main point.

Bad Example:
> I did the homework yesterday, but my dog ate it. So, I cannot submit it today.

Good Example:
> I can't submit the homework today. Actually, I did it yesterday, but my dog ate it.

## Transition Words

Use transition words to make your intention clear.

Example:
> I can build software for you. However, I cannot guarantee that it will be bug-free.

In that example, the word "However" is the transition word because it connects two sentences.

Transition words usually contain a comma (,) at the end.

## No Dense Information

Take a look at this example:
> GitHub is a cloud-based developer platform where software engineers store, manage, track, and collaborate on computer code.

Because GitHub has many features, this sentence tries to capture everything in one sentence.
However, don't cram too much information into one sentence.
Please simplify the information by removing everything except one essential point for the target audience.
You may give a hint that there is other information and explain it separately.
In this case, let’s say the target audience is a developer who wants to learn what they can do with GitHub.

Here is a better version:
> GitHub is a cloud platform where software developers can share their code. Furthermore, it offers many collaboration features, such as ticketing and a code review system. For example, your coworker can issue a ticket to report a bug. Also, your coworker can request a code review for their task.

## Abbreviation

Always explain the abbreviation the first time you use it in a document. In other words, write out the full phrase and introduce the abbreviation in parentheses. For all subsequent uses in the same document, you can use just the abbreviation.

Example:
> If you receive a Looks Good to Me (LGTM) comment from your peer, your work is usually ready for production. However, you must address AI review feedback even if you got an LGTM from your peer.
